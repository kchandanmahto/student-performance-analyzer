
# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

StudentIQ is a data science project developed under the **CKMHTO.AI** brand.

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and professional environment for everyone participating in this project.

We welcome contributions from people of all experience levels and backgrounds.

## Expected Behaviour

All project contributors and community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Communicate clearly and constructively.
- Respect different opinions and perspectives.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on improving the project.
- Help maintain a collaborative environment.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Offensive or inappropriate language.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing malicious code or harmful content.
- Any behaviour that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

## Contribution Standards

When participating in StudentIQ:

- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Respect maintainers and contributors.
- Follow the repository contribution guidelines.
- Report security concerns responsibly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

Inappropriate behaviour may result in:

1. A warning.
2. Removal of inappropriate content.
3. Temporary restriction from project participation.
4. Permanent exclusion from project contributions in serious cases.

The response will depend on the nature and severity of the violation.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the project maintainer.
